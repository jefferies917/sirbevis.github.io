# Amity Circles

Some *16 years ago* there was a **Provincial initiative** to ensure that **Masonic widows** had somewhere to *socialise* and meet other widows. In that way they might not feel *too lonely*.

There were already **two such clubs** in the Province of Hampshire and Isle of Wight, and after the **Provincial Grand Master** visited one of them, he declared there should be such clubs *throughout the Province*.

I was at the meeting to launch the one set up in the **central part of Hampshire at Winchester**. Following this meeting I became the **Treasurer** of the club — *“The Acorn Club”*.  

Whilst this web-note is about the **Acorn Club**, I know that many of the others have **similar ideas** for their members. A list of clubs and their contacts is available on the Provincial Web Site:  
[Amity Group – The Provincial Grand Lodge of Hampshire & Isle of Wight](https://hantsiowfreemasons.org/members-area/amity-group/)  

If you want to know more, *contact them directly*. There is **no club meeting at Albion Place where Sir Bevis meet**.

---

Whilst started for **Masonic widows**, the Acorn Club now has members who are *“elderly”* who have a **connection to Freemasonry** — both *females and males*.  

*“Elderly”* is in quotes because at one stage the Acorn Club had a **widow in her 30’s**. She later remarried and moved away.  

**Elderly brethren attend**, and if they are married, *bring their wives along*.

---

Throughout the year the **Acorn Club** has a *series of events*, most are **coffee mornings**. These start with a **cup of coffee or tea with biscuits**, followed by a *talk or demonstration*, and then a **light lunch**.  

The charge to the members is **£7**, and *in theory the club breaks even* on this.

There is a raffle — *nicknamed the “Con”*. The members donate the prizes and buy raffle tickets, then **win back what they have donated**!  

The proceeds from the raffle:
- **Cover the cost of speakers**
- **Subsidise outings**
- **Support the annual dinner**

---

There are **summer outings** and a trip to a **pantomime in December**. At the panto, the members are *quite vocal* at the appropriate times:  

*“He’s behind you!”*  
*“Oh no there isn’t!”*  
*“Oh yes there is!”*

The Acorn Club has also joined with other **Amity Circles**, for example on a **boat trip around the Isle of Wight**.  

*I think a good time is had by all.*

---

If you know, locally, an **elderly freemason** or a **widow**, *why not tell them about these clubs?* As mentioned, there is a **full list on the Provincial website**.  

Note also that other **Masonic Provinces** run similar clubs.

If you can help in some way — *for example*:
- Being a **driver** for a member without transport  
- Giving a **talk or demonstration**  

*Why not contact a club?*
