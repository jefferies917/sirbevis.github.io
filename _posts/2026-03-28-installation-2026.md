Congratulations to our **newly installed Master**, Worshipful Brother Lloyd Temple, who was duly installed for his second year in the chair during a well-attended and *enjoyable Installation meeting*.
<!-- more -->

And a further **congrats to all of the Officers** who were installed and who will support him throughout the year ahead. Sir Bevis is looking forward with *confidence and enthusiasm* to another great year.

The Officers installed for this year are:
- Worshipful Master - W. Bro. Lloyd Temple
- Senior Warden - Bro. K. Roberts
- Junior Warden - W. Bro. Duncan Kyle
- Senior Deacon - Bro. T. Cox
- Junior Deacon - Bro. M. Smith
- Inner Guard - Bro. E. Bempah
- Tyler - W. Bro. K. Webb

And also a massive congrats to W. Bro. Stuart Rowe who became an **honorary member**! Stuart has dedicated his life to Freemasonry and has been a member of Sir Bevis for decades, mentoring *hundreds if not thousands* of Masons along the way, very well deserved.

Sir Bevis Lodge wishes all Officers a **successful and rewarding year** in office and looks forward to the many meetings and events to come.
