# Freemasonry FAQs

## What is Freemasonry?

**Freemasonry** is one of the world’s oldest fraternal organisations. It brings together men of good character who seek to **improve themselves**, support **charitable causes**, and enjoy the **company of like-minded individuals**.

At its heart, Freemasonry is about:
- **Friendship** – building lasting relationships  
- **Charity** – supporting those in need  
- **Integrity** – striving to be better in everyday life  

It uses *ceremony and symbolism* to teach moral lessons, but it is **not a religion**, nor does it replace one.

---

## How do I join?

The traditional phrase is: *“To be one, ask one.”*  

This simply means that if you are interested, the first step is to **speak to a Freemason** or contact a local Lodge.

From there:
1. You’ll have an informal chat to learn more  
2. You may be invited to meet members of the Lodge  
3. If you wish to proceed, you can submit an application  

Freemasonry is open to men over 18 who are of **good character** and **genuine interest**.

If you don’t know a Mason, don’t worry — you can still **reach out via our website**, and we’ll guide you through the process.

---

## Is it expensive?

Freemasonry is **not intended to be exclusive or unaffordable**, but there are some costs involved.

These typically include:
- A **joining fee** (one-off)  
- An **annual subscription**  
- The cost of **meals or social events**  

There are also opportunities to give to **charity**, but this is always **voluntary**.

We aim to keep costs **reasonable and transparent**, and we are always happy to discuss them openly before you join.

---

## What do I bring to Lodge?

Besides yourself — and hopefully a little **money for charity** and a **drink at the bar** — all that is required is:

- The proper *[dress code]({{ '/dress-code/' | relative_url }})*  
- A **positive attitude** and willingness to take part  
- A **safe way to get home** after the evening  

Everything else will be explained and guided by the members of the Lodge. You won’t be expected to know anything in advance.

---

## What happens at a Lodge meeting?

A typical Lodge meeting includes:
- A **formal ceremony** (which may involve welcoming new members or progressing existing ones)  
- **Administrative business**  
- Time for **socialising afterwards**, usually over a meal  

The atmosphere is a balance of **tradition and friendliness** — formal in structure, but relaxed and welcoming in spirit.

---

## Do I have to believe in God?

Yes — Freemasonry requires members to have a belief in a **Supreme Being**.  

However, it is **not tied to any one religion**, and members come from many different faiths and backgrounds. Religion and politics are **not discussed in Lodge**, ensuring harmony among members.

---

## Is Freemasonry secret?

Freemasonry isn't really a *"secret society"*, but rather a **society with secrets**.

- Our **values, principles, and charitable work** are open  
- Some **ceremonial elements** are kept private to preserve their meaning  

There is nothing in Freemasonry that conflicts with **law, family, or personal beliefs**.

---

## What time commitment is involved?

Freemasonry is designed to **fit around your life**, not take it over.

Most Lodges meet:
- Around **7–9 times per year**  
- With optional **social events** throughout the year  

You can be as involved as your time allows.

---

## Can I visit before joining?

Yes — in many cases, you can meet members informally before making any commitment.

We encourage anyone interested to:
- Come along to a **social event**  
- Meet members in a **relaxed setting**  
- Ask any questions you may have
